Demand forecasting

Holt-Winters triple exponential smoothing with weekly seasonality. Smoothing constants are grid-searched per SKU by minimising in-sample squared error; the band is a 95% prediction interval widened by the square root of the horizon.

—

Model

level_t = α(y_t − s_{t−7}) + (1−α)(level_{t−1} + trend_{t−1}) trend_t = β(level_t − level_{t−1}) + (1−β)·trend_{t−1} s_t = γ(y_t − level_t) + (1−γ)·s_{t−7} ŷ_{t+h} = level_t + h·trend_t + s_{t+h−7}

Additive seasonality is used rather than multiplicative: several SKUs have near-zero demand on some days, and multiplicative factors are undefined there.

Replenishment policy

A continuous-review (Q, R) policy. Safety stock accounts for variance in demand and in supplier lead time — ignoring the second term is the usual reason a 95% target service level delivers about 80%.

Expiry & waste prediction

For each perishable batch the model forecasts cumulative demand over the remaining shelf life and integrates the shortfall, giving an expected write-off in units and dollars rather than a binary expired/not-expired flag.

Anomaly detection

A plain z-score flags every weekend as anomalous, because weekends genuinely sell more. The series is therefore decomposed into trend and weekly seasonality first, and the residual is scored with a MAD-based robust z — the median and MAD are not dragged around by the very outliers being searched for.

Detected events

|robust z| ≥ 3.0

Model accuracy

Rolling-origin backtest. At six successive cut points the model is refitted on history only and asked for the next seven days, which are then compared with held-out actuals. Two baselines run on identical folds, because a forecast that cannot beat "same as last Tuesday" is not worth deploying.

Reading these numbers

MAPE is reported with zero-demand days excluded, where the percentage error is undefined. Bias is mean error: positive means the model under-forecasts, which for an inventory system is the more expensive direction to be wrong in. Skill is the honest headline — it measures the model against the baseline a planner would otherwise use, not against zero.
